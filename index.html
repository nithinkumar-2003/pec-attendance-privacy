PEC-Attendance Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 September 2026

PEC-Attendance ("the app") is an internal attendance and task-management app operated by Prakasam Engineering College, PEC ("we", "us") for its administrators, heads of department (HODs) and faculty. This policy explains what information the app collects, how it is used, and the choices you have.

The app is intended only for staff members who have been given an account by the institution. It is not directed at the general public or at children.

1. Information we collect

  • Account information: name, email address, phone number, role (admin, HOD or faculty), department, and password (stored only in hashed form on our servers).
  • Academic records entered by staff: student names, roll numbers, classes, subjects, timetables, attendance records, class swaps and reports.
  • Tasks: task titles, descriptions, due dates, assignees, status updates and comments.
  • Voice notes: if you choose to record a voice note on a task update, the app uses your device microphone to record it and uploads the audio to our server. The microphone is used only while you are actively recording.
  • Files you upload: Excel or CSV files used to bulk-import faculty, HOD or student lists.
  • Device and notification data: a push-notification token (from Firebase Cloud Messaging) so we can send you task and attendance notifications.
  • Sign-in session: an authentication token stored on your device so you stay signed in.

The app does not collect your location, contacts, photos or advertising ID, and it does not contain advertising.

2. How we use information

  • To sign you in and verify your identity (including one-time codes for password reset).
  • To record and report attendance, and to manage timetables and class swaps.
  • To create, assign and track tasks, including voice-note updates.
  • To send push notifications about tasks and attendance.
  • To keep the service secure and to fix problems.

3. Sharing

We do not sell personal information and do not share it with third parties for marketing. Information is visible only to authorised users of the institution according to their role. We use these service providers to run the app:

  • Google Firebase Cloud Messaging: to deliver push notifications (Firebase privacy information).
  • Our hosting provider: to store app data on our servers.

We may disclose information if required by law.

4. Security

All data is sent between the app and our servers over encrypted HTTPS connections. Access to data is restricted by user role.

5. Data retention

We keep account, attendance and task data for as long as the account is active and as required for the institution's academic record-keeping. When a staff account is removed, the associated personal data is deleted or anonymised, except where records must be kept for academic or legal reasons.

6. Your choices and rights

  • You can turn off notifications or microphone access at any time in your phone's settings.
  • You can ask to access, correct or delete your personal data, or to delete your account, by contacting us at the address below. See How to delete your account.

7. How to delete your PEC-Attendance account

To request deletion of your PEC-Attendance account and its associated data:

  1. Send an email to pec@prakasamec.com from the email address registered to your account.
  2. Use the subject line "PEC-Attendance account deletion" and include your full name and department.
  3. We will confirm your identity and process the request within 30 days, then reply to confirm the deletion.

You can also ask to delete only some of your data (for example, voice notes you have recorded) without deleting your account, using the same email address.

Data that is deleted: your account profile (name, email address, phone number), login credentials, push-notification tokens, and voice notes you have recorded.

Data that may be kept: attendance records and task history that form part of the institution's official academic records may be kept for as long as required for academic or legal record-keeping. Where kept, they are no longer linked to an active account.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date above shows when it was last changed.

9. Contact

For questions or data requests, email: pec@prakasamec.com
